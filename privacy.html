Privacy notice

This notice covers the owner’s personal Home Assistant Google Drive backup connection under the OAuth app display name calendar. It covers this backup client, not unrelated clients in the same Google Cloud project.

Data and purpose

The connection handles Google authorization tokens, backup files and file metadata needed to upload, list, download and remove the app’s backup files. The files may contain private Home Assistant configuration and household information. They are used for the owner’s backup and recovery workflow.

Access and storage

The drive.file permission limits Drive access to files created by or otherwise made available to the app. Home Assistant stores its connection credentials in its local configuration. Backups are stored in the owner’s selected local and Google Drive backup locations. Protection and retention depend on Home Assistant’s settings; encrypted backups require the recovery key to restore. This website does not receive those tokens or backups.

Use and sharing

The backup connection uses Google data for backup and recovery, not advertising, sale of data or training general-purpose AI models. Data is transferred between the owner’s Home Assistant instance and Google Drive for this purpose. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google’s own service policies also apply to data held in Drive.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls backup schedules, destinations and retention in Home Assistant. Old app backups may be deleted automatically under those settings. Revoking authorization stops future access but does not itself erase existing backups. To delete stored data, remove the relevant backups in Home Assistant and Google Drive and remove the connection from Home Assistant.

Revoke access

Review or revoke the connection from Google Account connections. Contact the owner using the support address shown on the Google consent screen for questions about this personal configuration.

This website

These static pages contain no login form, analytics script or advertising. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Do not submit passwords, tokens or backup recovery keys here.
